Privacy Policy – Auto Refresh & Slow Load

Last updated: DATE_HERE

Auto Refresh & Slow Load ("the extension") does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information or browsing data.

What the extension does with your data

  • The extension keeps per-tab settings (whether refresh and slow load are on, the interval, the refresh count and the speed limit) in your browser's session storage. These settings stay on your device and are deleted when you close the browser.
  • The extension does not read, record or modify the content of the pages you visit or the data you send and receive.
  • The extension makes no network requests of its own and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • No data is sold, shared with or transferred to any third party.

Permissions

The extension requests access to all sites and the browser's debugging or request-handling features only so that the refresh timer and the download speed limit can work on the tab you choose. Nothing is collected through these permissions.
